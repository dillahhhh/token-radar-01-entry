What is each pre-token protocol worth, priced on the fitted curve?

The model. Each protocol is priced by one curve fitted to the 128 live tokens in this snapshot: ln(market cap) = 6.96 + 0.522 × ln(TVL). A protocol ten times larger in TVL is worth about 3.3× as much, not 10×.

The coverage. The board covers the 40 watchlist protocols — at least $50M of TVL with no token on record — plus 158 early ones below that floor: 198 rows.

The limit. This is a model estimate, not a price and not a promise: on tokens it had not seen, the typical prediction missed by 90%. The validation panel below shows the miss, not just the fit.
