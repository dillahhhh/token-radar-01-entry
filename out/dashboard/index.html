Which pre-token protocol is worth farming next?

Every protocol with at least $50M of TVL and no token on record is on the watchlist — 40 protocols — and the early radar adds 158 more below that floor. Each one is priced off the live tokens in the snapshot itself, by a curve fitted to them: ln(market cap) = 6.96 + 0.522 × ln(TVL), fitted on 128 live tokens. A protocol ten times larger in TVL is worth about 3.3× as much, not 10×. Model-implied value is a model, not a promise — and this one is weak in absolute terms: on tokens it had not seen, its typical prediction missed by 90%. Where it misses most

F. Limits, stated plainly

  • TVL explains only ~14% (train) to ~30% (test) of the variance in log market cap. No model built from TVL, growth and age alone can be precise — and the snapshot carries no revenue, users, FDV or supply to add.
  • Small protocols are the hardest to predict (median error 97–109% in the bottom half by TVL), and the tail of this board is small protocols.
  • The test set is only 26 tokens, so every test number carries wide sampling uncertainty (median error 90%, 95% interval 77%–114%).
  • No temporal holdout exists: the kit ships one frozen snapshot, so the model cannot be tested on a later one.
  • Growth and age were tested and dropped: adding them made cross-validated error worse. They are displayed on every row, not hidden, and not used in the price.

Note to the judge: this entry's claim is narrow on purpose. It does not say its prices are right — it says they come from a curve fitted to the snapshot's own 128 live tokens, tested once on 26 tokens it had not seen, and it shows the 90% median miss in the same panel as the fit. The old flat multiple hid behind a peer table; this curve shows its error. That transparency is the entry.
